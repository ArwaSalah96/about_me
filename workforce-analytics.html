PROJECT OVERVIEW

Understanding the workforce data.

This project uses a fictional dataset representing employees of Square Architecture Solutions, a fictional company. The dataset covers the years 2023 and 2024 and was created to demonstrate how employee-related financial and leave information can be consolidated into a structured HR reporting solution.

The dashboard brings together employee compensation information from different sources, allowing users to review employee-level figures as well as overall trends across the reporting period.

All company names and employee information used in this project are fictional and do not represent real individuals or an actual organization.

REPORT STRUCTURE

What does the dashboard cover?

01 · Employee Compensation Overview

The first report page provides a consolidated view of employee financial information, including:

  • Monthly salaries and hourly rates.
  • Daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly compensation figures.
  • Bonuses and their breakdown.
  • Deductions and related amounts.
  • Regular leave payments, national holiday leave payments, and maternity leave payments where applicable.

02 · Compensation & Leave Analysis

The second report page uses charts to explore changes and comparisons over time, including:

  • Changes in each employee's hourly rate throughout the period.
  • Annual leave utilization by employee.
  • Employees receiving the highest bonuses.
  • Employees with the highest deductions.

DATA PREPARATION

Bringing scattered data together.

One of the main challenges in this project was preparing the data before it could be used reliably in the report. The information was collected from multiple Excel and CSV files, which required considerable effort to consolidate and review.

I used Microsoft Excel and Power Query to clean, organize, and prepare the data. The preparation process involved:

01

Data Collection

Gathering employee-related information from multiple Excel spreadsheets and CSV files.

02

Data Cleaning

Using Excel and Power Query to organize the data, address inconsistencies, and prepare it for analysis.

03

Validation & Reconciliation

Comparing figures across different sources and checking the information against additional references to improve accuracy and consistency.

Reliable reporting starts with reliable data. Data preparation and validation were essential parts of this project, not just preliminary technical steps.

THE ROLE OF DATA ANALYSIS

Turning employee records into meaningful insights.

Employee information is often spread across different files and records. Data analysis helps transform these separate records into a consolidated view that makes financial patterns, changes, and comparisons easier to understand.

Data Consolidation

Combining information from different sources gives users a more complete view of employee-related financial data.

Trend Analysis

Tracking hourly rate changes, bonuses, deductions, and leave utilization helps reveal how workforce figures change over time.

Data Accuracy

Cleaning and reconciling information helps reduce inconsistencies and provides a more dependable foundation for reporting.

Clearer Comparisons

Visual reports make it easier to compare employees, review compensation components, and identify notable differences.

BUSINESS VALUE

How can HR and managers benefit?

A consolidated workforce dashboard can support HR teams and managers by making employee-related financial information easier to review, monitor, and communicate. Instead of manually collecting figures from multiple files, users can access a structured reporting view.

For HR Teams

The dashboard supports HR teams in reviewing employee compensation records, monitoring leave utilization, and comparing bonuses and deductions. It also provides a clearer overview of employee-related financial information across different periods.

For Managers

Managers can use the dashboard to understand workforce cost patterns, review changes in hourly rates, and gain visibility into compensation-related trends that may support planning and management discussions.

For Finance Teams

The consolidated view can make it easier to review compensation components, compare figures across periods, and coordinate with HR when reviewing employee-related financial information.

For Decision-Makers

Visual summaries and employee-level details provide a more accessible basis for investigating unusual figures, understanding workforce cost patterns, and supporting data-informed decisions.

The dashboard is a reporting and analysis tool. It supports review and decision-making, but does not replace payroll processing, HR systems, or formal financial approval procedures.

PROJECT VALUE

From scattered spreadsheets to structured HR reporting.

This project demonstrates how data preparation, validation, and business intelligence can turn scattered employee records into a consolidated and interactive workforce analytics solution.

By bringing compensation and leave information together, the dashboard provides a clearer view of employee-related financial data across 2023 and 2024. It makes detailed information easier to explore while giving HR teams, managers, and other relevant stakeholders a more accessible way to review workforce trends.
